What is SouqNote?

SouqNote is a lightweight inventory and sales management app designed for small businesses, shop owners, and online sellers. It helps you track products, sales, and credits easily without complicated setup or unnecessary features.

Key Features

Inventory Tracking

Manage products, stock levels, and prices in one place.

Sales Recording

Record daily sales quickly and keep clear transaction history.

Credit Management

Track customer credits and outstanding payments with ease.

Offline First

Your data is stored locally on your device. No internet required.

Pricing

SouqNote is currently available as a free app. Additional features may be introduced in the future.
